
## Naming the sense you translated

Alongside each Vietnamese sense, give the English sense it translates.

That English is not a copy of the WordNet definition you were given — it is
that definition condensed to at most eight words. Several WordNet definitions
may collapse into one sense; when they do, name what you actually merged.

    {"en": "season between winter and summer", "vi": "mùa xuân"}
    {"en": "coiled elastic device", "vi": "lò xo"}
    {"en": "natural flow of ground water", "vi": "suối, mạch nước ngầm"}

The two halves must describe the same sense. This is what lets a reviewer tell
which English meaning a Vietnamese line is answering — `gloss_en` cannot, being
WordNet's list in WordNet's order — so a mismatched pair is worse than none.
